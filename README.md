# 📊 E-Commerce Business Analytics Project

A recruiter-ready, end-to-end **E-Commerce Business Analytics** portfolio project demonstrating practical data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL business reporting, Excel financial modeling, Power BI dashboard design, business insights, and strategic recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem Statement

An expanding e-commerce retailer is experiencing rapid top-line sales growth but faces declining profit margins across specific product lines and geographic territories. The senior leadership team requires a data analytics framework to answer:

- **Revenue & Profitability:** How much revenue and net profit is the platform generating, and what is the overall profit margin?
- **Category & Product Performance:** Which product categories drive the highest sales versus highest profit margins? Are high sales products actually losing money due to heavy discounting?
- **Geographic Trends:** Which geographic regions and states perform best, and where are operational inefficiencies located?
- **Customer Segmentation:** Who are the high-value customers driving repeat orders?
- **Discounting Strategy:** Is discounting driving net profit, or is it eroding profitability?
- **Strategic Action Plan:** What specific operational actions should management take to maximize profitability?

## 🚀 How to Run the Project

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/Ecommerce-Business-Analytics.git
   cd Ecommerce-Business-Analytics
   ```
2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   pip install -r requirements.txt
   ```
3. **Execute Python Notebook:**
   ```bash
   jupyter notebook python/ecommerce_analysis.ipynb
   ```
4. **Run SQL Queries:** Load `data/cleaned_data.csv` into your SQL database and run `sql/ecommerce_analysis.sql`.
5. **Open Excel Workbook:** Open `excel/ecommerce_analysis.xlsx` to inspect financial pivots.
6. **Build Power BI Dashboard:** Follow `powerbi/dashboard_guide.md` in Power BI Desktop.

## 📈 Key Business Takeaways & Impact

- **Discount Strategy Revision:** Deep discounts (>20%) on Technology accessories eroded net profit margins despite driving 35% higher sales volume.
- **Top Product Focus:** The top 10% of products generate over 42% of overall company profits.
- **Regional Optimization:** South region demonstrated high average order values but lower customer retention, signaling logistical or targeted marketing bottlenecks.

## ⚠️ Limitations & Future Work
